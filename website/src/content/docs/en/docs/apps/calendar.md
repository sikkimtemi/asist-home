ASIST shows and searches the events in Google Calendar, and can add, change and delete ordinary events. It works on a Mac and on Windows. How to connect it is covered in [Connecting your calendar](/en/docs/start/calendar/).

Under "Calendar" in the Dock, you can switch between Month, Week and Schedule. In the conversation, ask things like "Show me today's schedule" or "Put a meeting in at 10 tomorrow".

Writes, whether from the conversation or from the screen, happen after you approve them on a confirmation screen, and are saved straight to Google Calendar. Change and delete recurring events and events with invitations in Google Calendar. Events changed on another device or in Google Calendar are read again the next time ASIST shows events. Spoken reminders also refresh their events periodically.

## Spoken calendar reminders

Under Calendar and mail in settings, enable "Spoken calendar reminders" to periodically read the calendars selected for display. Select your family calendar for family reminders. "Minutes before the event" defaults to 30 and accepts 1–1440 minutes. Set "Refresh interval (seconds)" to 60 during development and 1800 (30 minutes) for daily use. Events already fetched are scheduled independently of the refresh interval. Additions, changes and cancellations take effect after the next refresh.

Each reminder announces every timed event still to start today, including events later than the one that triggered it. With A's lesson at 17:30 and B's lesson at 19:30, the announcement at 17:00 includes both; the one at 19:00 includes only B's lesson. All-day events and events already started are excluded. Reminders due together share one announcement.

The feed labels the input "Automatically generated by the scheduler". Your conversation model words the announcement naturally, including appropriate forms of address, and your existing voice reads it. Reminders require a connection to your model provider and available usage; event details are sent to that provider. With speech disabled, replies appear only as text. A running conversation takes priority; if an event starts while its reminder waits, that reminder is skipped.

When a reminder falls due while the window is minimized or hidden in the tray, it is skipped. Returning to the window shows "Some scheduled reminders could not be delivered on time." It does not display the missed events or replay their announcements. Reminders cannot run while ASIST is fully quit or the computer is asleep.

Automatic inputs are deduplicated across restarts. An uncertain delivery after a crash or failed response is not retried, so an announcement can be lost. Refresh and persistence errors appear in settings. Failed refreshes pause announcements based on stale calendar data until a refresh succeeds.
